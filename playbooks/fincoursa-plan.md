# Fincoursa go-to-market plan: $199 per module, modelled on Wall Street Prep

Status: **proposal.** Prices other than the $199 module are recommendations. Wall Street
Prep (WSP) figures come from search results on 2026-10-04; the WSP site itself could not be
opened from here, so re-check them before quoting any publicly.

## 1. What to copy from Wall Street Prep, and what not to

WSP's model in one line: **free content → cheap entry products → bundle "for the price of
one course" → live bootcamps → university co-branded certificates, with corporate and
university licences running alongside.**

| WSP does | Price (reported) | Fincoursa equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| A huge SEO knowledge base (definitions, Excel how-tos, "is X worth it?") | Free | Technical analysis and markets knowledge base (§4) |
| 7 free lessons + a free Red Book sample PDF | Free | 3 free lessons per track + cheat-sheet PDFs |
| Low-cost entry products (Excel Crash Course, Red Book) | $39 / $99 | One $49 "starter" mini-course |
| Single specialist courses | $399–499 | **Modules at $199** |
| Premium Package: 7 courses, lifetime access, certification | $499 (≈ one course) | **Track bundle with certificate** (§2) |
| Verifiable certificate on its own domain, shareable to LinkedIn | Included | Verifiable credential + LinkedIn share (§3) |
| Live bootcamps, also run through CFA societies | $2,499–2,999 | Live cohorts, run through **CMT chapters** |
| University co-branded certificates (Wharton, Columbia) | ~$5,000 | Year-two goal: a co-branded certificate with a university or professional body |
| Corporate training (300+ firms) + student "Passport" via universities | ~$200–300/seat to schools | Team licences + student-club licences (§5) |
| Customer referral codes + creator affiliates with coupon codes | Rate not published | Affiliate tiers (§6) |

**Don't copy WSP's core subject head-on.** Financial modelling (3-statement, DCF, LBO) is
crowded by WSP, CFI, and BIWS. Lead with what your instructors are uniquely credible in:
**technical analysis and markets** (CMT-aligned), derivatives, FX, and commodities. CMT exam
prep supply looks thin: the UWorld CMT listings are marked discontinued and a Kaplan CMT
product wasn't found. **Check that gap before building on it.** Add modelling later only
if you recruit strong instructors for it.

**Trademark caution:** "CMT" and "Chartered Market Technician" belong to the CMT
Association. Don't name products "CMT Prep" or imply endorsement without permission. Ask
the Association whether it has an approved-provider or partner program. Until then, use
descriptive wording ("technical analysis, aligned to the CMT body of knowledge") only if
the Association's rules allow it.

## 2. Product and price ladder

| Rung | What | Price | Access | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Free | 3 free lessons per track, cheat sheets, templates, knowledge base, monthly webinar | $0 | — | Lead capture (email) |
| 1. Starter | 60–90 minute mini-course (e.g. "Reading a chart in 90 minutes") | $49 | Lifetime | Turns a lead into a buyer cheaply |
| 2. **Module** | One topic, 3–6 hours of video, exercises, quiz | **$199** | Lifetime (WSP standard) or 12 months (decide) | Core unit; one instructor each |
| 3. **Track + certificate** | 5–6 modules + final exam + verified certificate | **$599** (list $995–1,194 bought separately) | Lifetime | Main revenue product, like WSP's Premium Package |
| 4. All-Access | Every module and track, new modules as released, monthly live Q&A | **$799/yr** | 12 months, renewing | Recurring revenue, like CFI's $497–847/yr |
| 5. Live cohort | 6 weeks, live classes with an instructor, office hours, capstone | $1,499 | Cohort + lifetime replay | Premium price; run through CMT chapters and partners |
| 6. Institutional | Team licences, student-club licences, co-branded certificates | §5 | — | Volume and credibility |

**Upgrade credit is essential with per-module pricing.** Anyone who bought modules can
upgrade to the track for *$599 minus what they already paid*. This is automated (§7). It
removes the "should I wait for the bundle?" hesitation that kills module sales.

### Example tracks (built from the instructors you have)

1. **Technical Analysis Professional:** chart foundations; trend, support and resistance;
   indicators and oscillators; patterns; intermarket and breadth; risk management and
   position sizing; plus a final exam.
2. **Derivatives and Options Strategist:** options basics, the Greeks, volatility,
   spreads, hedging, and crypto derivatives.
3. **Global Macro, FX and Commodities:** FX drivers, commodities cycles, indices and
   intermarket analysis, and macro calendars.
4. Later: **Financial Modelling** and **Portfolio and Risk for Advisers** (RIA-facing,
   with CE credit).

## 3. Certification (WSP's real moat)

- **A verified credential** for each track: a public verification page per holder, a
  one-click "Add to LinkedIn" button, and a unique ID. Use Accredible, Credly, or your
  LMS's certificate feature, on a subdomain like `certificates.fincoursa.com`.
- **The exam must be real:** a timed, proctored-light test with a pass mark. The
  certificate is only worth something if people can fail.
- **Continuing-education credits** raise both price and B2B demand. NASBA CPE sponsor
  registration (WSP has it) and CFP Board CE are worth applying for in year one, for the
  adviser-facing tracks.
- **SEO support,** as WSP does: publish "Is the Fincoursa certificate worth it?" and "Best
  technical analysis certifications in 2027" pages. Be honest and include competitors.

## 4. Marketing engine

1. **Knowledge base:**
   - Definition and how-to pages for technical indicators, chart patterns, options
     strategies, FX and commodities terms. These are high-volume search topics (RSI, MACD,
     Fibonacci, head and shoulders, the Greeks…).
   - Each page carries an instructor byline, a worked chart example, a free-lesson CTA,
     and the risk disclaimer.
   - Quality over volume: 3–5 instructor-reviewed pages a week, not hundreds of AI pages.
     Google treats mass-produced pages as scaled content abuse.
2. **Free lessons + cheat-sheet PDFs** as email lead magnets, one per track.
3. **Monthly free webinar** taught by a rotating instructor. It acquires new leads and is
   also a perk for All-Access members (WSP does both).
4. **Instructor-led social:** each instructor posts 2–3 short chart lessons a week
   (YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn, X), linking to their free lesson with their own affiliate
   tag.
5. **Comparison and "worth it" pages:** Fincoursa vs other technical analysis courses,
   honest and factual.
6. **Cross-sell with NordStar Pro:**
   - Each module ends with "see this method applied every week", linking to a NordStar Pro
     trial.
   - NordStar Pro members get 20% off modules.
   - A "Learn + Apply" bundle: track + 3 months of research.

## 6. Affiliates for Fincoursa

Assumptions: a $199 module nets about $193 after card fees, and a $599 track about $581.

| Sale | Platform, no affiliate (50%) | With affiliate (30% of net off the top) |
|---|---|---|
| $199 module | Platform $96 / instructor $96 | Affiliate $58 / platform $67 / instructor $67 |
| $599 track | Platform $291 / instructor $291 | Affiliate $174 / platform $203 / instructor $203 |
| $799 All-Access (year 1) | Platform $388 / instructor pool $388 | Affiliate $233 / platform $271 / pool $271 |

| Tier | Commission (proposal) | Audience discount |
|---|---|---|
| Instructors (own audience) | Channel split 70/30 in the instructor's favour (see `marketplace-sales-and-affiliates.md`) | Up to 15% |
| Finance creators | 30% of net (CFI reportedly pays up to 15% and gives 30% off; a higher commission with a smaller discount is the better recruiting pitch) | 10–15% |
| Customers (referral code) | $40 credit per referred purchase, calculated on retail price as WSP does | 10% |
| Student ambassadors (one per campus club) | 20% + free All-Access while active | Club code |
| CMT chapters and partners | Revenue share or a per-head fee on co-hosted cohorts | Member price |

Same rules as NordStar Pro: affiliate disclosure, no earnings or performance claims,
approved copy only, no brand-keyword bidding, 30-day cookie, payouts after the refund
window, and clawbacks on refunds.

### How to split multi-instructor products

- **Track:** split the instructor half across the modules' owners in proportion to each
  module's list price.
- **All-Access:** pool the instructor half and split it monthly by each instructor's share
  of lessons completed or minutes watched (the standard subscription-marketplace method).
- **Live cohorts:** the instructor does live work, so consider 60/40 in the instructor's
  favour.
- Write all of this into the instructor contract.

## 7. Automation (the agent drafts; a human approves anything public or financial)

| Trigger | Automated action |
|---|---|
| Free lesson signup | 6-email sequence over 14 days: lesson → cheat sheet → instructor story → module offer → webinar invite → track offer |
| Abandoned checkout | 2 reminders (1 hour, 24 hours), then stop |
| Module purchased | Welcome + "start here"; at 50% progress an encouragement email; at completion the next module and a track upgrade-credit offer |
| 2+ modules owned | Upgrade-credit email: "finish the track for $X more" |
| Track completed | Exam invite → certificate issued → LinkedIn share prompt → review request → NordStar Pro trial → referral code |
| 14 days with no progress | Nudge with the next lesson and a webinar invite |
| All-Access renewal in 30 days / 7 days | Reminder with "what you used this year" summary |
| Club or team enquiry | Agent researches the organisation, drafts a proposal and licence quote; human sends |
| Weekly | Agent drafts 3–5 knowledge-base pages for instructor review, social clips from lessons, and affiliate prospect pitches |
| Monthly | Instructor revenue statements (per-module, track, pool), affiliate payout report, cohort and webinar calendar. **Humans pay.** |

**Where it runs:** Fincoursa_LMS is a UI prototype with no backend. Until a backend is
built, sell on **Thinkific**, which supports bundles, coupons, certificates, and an
affiliate feature depending on plan. Run sequences in Kit or Thinkific's email tools, and
attach credentials through an Accredible or Credly integration. The agent's playbooks are
the same either way.

## 8. Launch plan (first 120 days)

| Weeks | Do |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Decide access (lifetime vs 12 months), the track price, the All-Access price, refund policy (e.g. 14 days, under 20% consumed), and the instructor contract (splits, pooling). Ask the CMT Association about trademark use and partnership. |
| 2–4 | Pick **one** track (Technical Analysis Professional). Line up 2–3 instructors and outline 6 modules. Build the free lessons and cheat sheets. Open a waitlist. |
| 4–8 | Pre-sell a **founding cohort**: the track at a founding price, with live Q&A, to the waitlist and instructors' audiences. Record modules as the cohort runs (pre-selling validates demand before full production). |
| 6–12 | Launch modules at $199 and the track at $599 on Thinkific. Start the knowledge base (3–5 pages a week), the monthly webinar, and the email sequences. Recruit the first 10–20 creator affiliates. |
| 10–16 | Certificate live with LinkedIn sharing. Pitch 3 CMT chapters for co-hosted cohorts and 5–10 university clubs for student licences. Start the NordStar Pro cross-sell. |
| 16–17 | Review: revenue by rung (module / track / All-Access / B2B) and by channel (organic / instructor / affiliate / B2B). Decide on track 2 and on applying for CPE/CE credits. |

### Targets to judge it by (assumptions; adjust after the founding cohort)

- Free-lesson signup → any purchase within 60 days: 3–5%
- Module buyers who upgrade to the track: 20–30%
- Founding cohort: 30–50 buyers from instructor audiences and the waitlist
