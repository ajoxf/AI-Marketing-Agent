- **Google's position:** AI-assisted content is allowed. Mass-produced pages meant to
  manipulate rankings are "scaled content abuse", whoever wrote them. Spam policies now
  also cover AI Overviews and AI Mode.
  [Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content)
- **GEO trend:** the #1 result loses about 58% of its CTR when an AI Overview appears
  (Ahrefs), and about 68% of searches are zero-click. Getting cited by AI answers through
  original data, named experts, clear structure, and third-party mentions now matters as
  much as ranking. NordStar Pro's weekly original research fits this well.
  [Medianama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/02/223-google-ai-overviews-click-through-rates-58-study/) ·
  [SEL](https://searchengineland.com/google-zero-click-searches-2026-study-479717)

## Ads

- **Google Search** on high-intent terms beats Performance Max on small budgets.
- **Meta Advantage+:** a Haus analysis of 640 incrementality tests found it
  underperformed manual campaigns over time despite strong reported ROAS.
- Judge ads by new-paid-member CPA with holdouts, not by the ROAS the platform reports.
- **Financial and crypto ad policies apply.** Check eligibility before any spend.
- [Pixis](https://pixis.ai/blog/advantage-vs-performance-max-head-to-head-2026/) ·

## Voice

Vapi, Retell, and Bland all run about $0.07–0.31 per minute. Only inbound support is
worth considering. **No outbound AI calling:** the FCC's 2024 ruling treats AI voices as
"artificial voice" under TCPA, so these calls need prior express written consent, and
penalties are $500–1,500 per call.
[Henson Legal](https://www.henson-legal.com/newsroom/ai-voice-tcpa-compliance-guide)

## Compliance checklist (applies to everything the agent does)

- **Email authentication:** Gmail, Yahoo, and Microsoft require SPF, DKIM, and DMARC
  (aligned), one-click unsubscribe, opt-outs honoured within about 2 days, and spam
  complaints under 0.3%.
  [mailrisk](https://mailrisk.io/guides/gmail-yahoo-sender-requirements-2026)
- **CAN-SPAM:** postal address, working opt-out, and honest subject lines.
- **GDPR / PECR:** B2C marketing email needs consent. B2B can rely on documented
  legitimate interest.
  [knowlee](https://www.knowlee.ai/blog/gdpr-compliant-cold-email-2026)
- **EU AI Act Art. 50** (live since 2026-08-02): chatbots must disclose they are AI from
  the first message, and synthetic media must be labelled.
  [darwinapps](https://www.darwinapps.com/blog/eu-ai-act-article-50-for-marketing-teams-what-to-disclose-after-2-august-2026/)
- **Investment-research specific:** stay impersonal (US publisher's exclusion, *Lowe v.
  SEC*), make no performance promises (FTC), and check UK/EU financial-promotion rules if
  marketing there. **Get legal sign-off.**
